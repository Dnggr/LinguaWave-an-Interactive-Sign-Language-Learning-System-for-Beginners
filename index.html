Step-by-Step Flow

How LinguaWave works

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Register with email and password. Choose your starting proficiency level.

  2. 02

    Pick a lesson

    Your dashboard shows available lessons and your progress so far.

  3. 03

    Learn the sign

    View the image, read the description, then watch the short video demo.

  4. 04

    Take the quiz

    Answer multiple-choice questions. Score ≥80% to unlock the next lesson.

  5. 05

    Track progress & give feedback

    Your dashboard updates automatically. Complete a short survey after each level.
