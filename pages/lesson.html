How to form Letter F

Touch the tip of your index finger to the tip of your thumb, forming a circle. Keep your middle, ring, and pinky fingers extended straight up and slightly spread apart. Hold your hand upright with your palm facing forward.

  • ✦ Index finger and thumb form an "OK" circle
  • ✦ Three remaining fingers point up
  • ✦ Palm faces the person you're signing to
