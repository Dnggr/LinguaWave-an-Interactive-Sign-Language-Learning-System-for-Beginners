How to perform the sign

Make a fist with your thumb resting against the side of your index finger, not tucked underneath. Hold your hand upright with your palm facing the person you're signing to.

  1. Close all fingers into a firm fist
  2. Rest your thumb against the side of your index finger
  3. Keep your wrist straight and hand relaxed
