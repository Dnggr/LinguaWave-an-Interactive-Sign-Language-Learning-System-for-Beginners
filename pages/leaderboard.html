Leaderboard

Earn XP from lessons and the Wall Breaker game. Lessons are worth far more than games, so there is no shortcut to the top.

How XP works

  • Every lesson step earns XP once. Finishing a whole lesson and passing its Mastery Quiz pays a bonus (the largest reward).
  • Wall Breaker earns a little XP, and less each wall you clear in a day. It stops paying after the daily limit.
  • A wall only counts when at least 6 of its bricks are signs you have already learned in lessons.
  • Streaks count days you earn XP, using your own time zone. Week rankings reset every Monday (UTC).
  • Each level has a lesson badge and a wall badge. Earn them by finishing a lesson or a wall once you reach that level.
