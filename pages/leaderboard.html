How XP works

  • Every lesson step earns XP once. Finishing a whole lesson and passing its Mastery Quiz pays a bonus (the largest reward).
  • Construct a Sentence, Wall Breaker and Time Attack earn a little XP each. Together they stop paying after the daily game limit.
  • Construct a Sentence is open to everyone: you can earn XP, levels and badges from it without finishing any lessons first.
  • Wall Breaker needs at least 6 learned bricks. A Time Attack word only pays XP when you have learned every letter and number in it.
  • The Level board ranks learners by level, and XP breaks ties. Game Leaderboards are separate: they rank your fastest completed run (not XP) in Construct, Time Attack and Wall Breaker.
  • Streaks count days you earn XP, using your own time zone.
  • Each level has a lesson badge, a wall badge and a sentence badge. Earn them by finishing a lesson, a wall or a sentence run once you reach that level.
